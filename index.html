💡 Whiteboard Tips for Responders

1. Keep Facts and Guesses Separate

Log evidence (Observed Event) is a hard Fact. Working ideas ("Is this C2?") are Decisions or notes. Keep them distinct so team members don't jump to conclusions.

2. Retroactive Attack Events

If log analysis reveals an attack started days ago, log it under Phase "0. Occurrence / Latency" so your timeline accurately reflects when the attacker first got in.

3. Privacy First (Zero Leaks)

Everything stays inside your browser's local memory. No data or telemetry leaves your computer, making it completely safe for sensitive incident notes.
